Privacy Policy

Wadyret · Last updated October 14, 2026

Wadyret is a private iPhone app for framing photos with morpho crystal bezels, filters, stickers, and captions. This policy explains what the app uses and how it stays on your device. By using Wadyret, you agree to this policy.

Who we are

Wadyret is published as a local iPhone application (bundle ID com.wadyret.app). There is no Wadyret account and no Wadyret cloud sync. For privacy questions, contact wadyret.studio@gmail.com.

Information stored on this iPhone

Depending on how you use the app, Wadyret may store photos you capture or import, framing drafts, finished creations, captions, sticker placements, filter settings, and your Wing purchase ledger in the app’s private container on this device. Payment card details for purchases are handled by Apple, not by Wadyret forms.

Permissions

Wadyret asks for system permissions only when you use the related feature. Declining a permission keeps browsing frames and saved drafts available.

  • Camera — when you tap Take Photo to capture a picture for a butterfly frame.
  • Photos — when you tap Import from Photos to choose a picture, or tap Save to Photos to export a finished frame to your library.
  • Microphone and speech recognition — when you tap the microphone so spoken words can become a caption or a search phrase after you confirm them.
  • Notifications — only if you allow local reminders about unfinished drafts or finished renders.

Wadyret does not request tracking permission and does not use advertising SDKs to track you across other apps.

Purchases

Optional Wing packs are consumable in-app purchases sold through Apple. Apple processes payment under Apple’s policies. Wadyret keeps a local ledger so your Wing balance is accurate and the same transaction is not granted twice. Unused Wings stay on this installation and are removed if you delete the app or use Clear All Data.

Sharing

Wadyret does not sell personal information and does not share your photos or captions with advertising networks. Apple may receive purchase and device information as part of App Store services. If you save a finished frame to Photos, or if you back up this iPhone, those copies follow Apple’s backup and Photos rules.

Retention and deletion

You can delete a single creation inside the app. Clear All Data permanently removes creations, drafts, media files, preferences, and unused Wings from this installation. Uninstalling Wadyret also removes its on-device store. Copies you already exported to Photos are not removed by the app.

Children

Wadyret is not directed to children under 13. Do not use the app to store photos of a child if you are not permitted to do so.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use of Wadyret after an update means you accept the revised policy. See also our Terms of Use.
